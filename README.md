# Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery (BCDR) Plan and Testing Tracker

An enterprise-ready *Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery (BCDR)* tracking template designed to help organizations map mission-critical assets, evaluate Business Impact Analysis (BIA) ratings, calculate Recovery Time Objectives (RTO) and Recovery Point Objectives (RPO), and track compliance testing cycles and remediation action items for audits like *ISO 27001* and *SOC 2*.

## 🚀 Key Features

* *Asset & Plan Mapping:* Tracks unique Plan IDs against core organizational processes and asset names.
* *Risk & Impact Metrics:* Categorizes operational disruption via Business Impact Analysis (BIA) ratings.
* *Recovery Objectives:* Clearly defines Recovery Time Objectives (RTO) and Recovery Point Objectives (RPO) for continuous business operations.
* *Compliance & Audit Readiness:* Logs operational ownership, last test dates, test statuses, re-test due schedules, and specific remediation action items.
